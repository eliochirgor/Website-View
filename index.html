The menu

Breakfast all day, lunch from the griddle, and a specials board that changes daily.

Three eggs, garlic butter, cheddar and pepper-and-onion hash browns on a long roll, with your choice of breakfast meat.

Cheesesteak Hangover

Shaved sirloin, three eggs, cheddar, hash browns, lettuce, tomato and onion on a long roll.

Veggie Hangover can be vegan

Three eggs, garlic butter, cheddar, hash browns, roasted red peppers and spinach. Ask for tofu instead of egg.

Fajita Hangover

Chicken or shaved sirloin with eggs, cheddar, hash browns and salsa on a garlic-buttered roll.

Fried egg sandwich

One, two or three eggs with cheddar on a bagel, bread, kaiser, English muffin or croissant. Swap in egg whites on anything.

Grab and go, or head upstairs.
